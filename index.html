How this figure is worked out

The calculator uses a standard repayment mortgage formula, which spreads both the capital you borrowed and the interest across equal monthly payments. Early payments are weighted more toward interest; later payments pay off more of the capital, even though the total payment stays the same throughout the term.

What changes your monthly payment most

Rate and term move the figure more than most people expect. Stretching a loan from 25 to 35 years lowers the monthly payment but usually increases the total interest paid by a large margin, because you're paying interest for an extra decade. A larger deposit reduces the loan amount directly, which lowers both the payment and the total interest.

What this calculator doesn't include

This tool estimates capital and interest only. It doesn't account for arrangement fees, early repayment charges, changes in interest rate after a fixed period ends, buildings insurance, or ground rent and service charges on leasehold properties. Treat the result as a starting estimate, not a mortgage offer.
